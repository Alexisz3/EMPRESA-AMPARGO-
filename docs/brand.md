# AMPARGO Brand Foundation

## Confirmed context

- Brand name: AMPARGO.
- Industry: remodeling and construction.
- Primary market: Houston, Texas.
- Primary commercial language: English.
- Secondary language: Spanish.
- Primary website conversion: quote request.
- Photography direction: real client work, including completed projects and selected progress imagery.

## Working positioning

Present AMPARGO as a capable, trustworthy local contractor through clear information and evidence of completed work. Copy should be confident and direct, with a practical homeowner-first tone.

## Voice principles

- Clear: use familiar language and explain what the visitor can expect.
- Specific: prefer approved project and service details over generic claims.
- Trustworthy: never invent credentials, figures, guarantees, or testimonials.
- Bilingual by design: write natural English and natural Spanish rather than translating word for word.
- Action-oriented: make the next step toward a quote easy to understand.

## Visual direction status

The current website color, typography, spacing, and image treatment form the approved working visual system. The final production logo and favicon remain pending.

The client supplied a photograph of an older business card with a house symbol, the letters A/P, “General Remodeling,” and “Andrade Parra Corporation.” It is visual reference only. Do not use the card photograph as the production logo. The confirmed website brand remains AMPARGO, and any recreated or adapted mark requires approval.

## Photography guidelines for the next phase

- Lead with strong, well-lit finished work where possible.
- Use process photos when they clarify craftsmanship or tell a meaningful project story.
- Avoid presenting unrelated images as a single before-and-after pair.
- Record project type, location granularity approved by the client, completion status, and alt text for every selected image.
- Do not use stock imagery as a substitute for real AMPARGO work unless explicitly approved and clearly appropriate.
